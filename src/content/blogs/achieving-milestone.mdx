# Build AI products people actually use

I'm Adewunmi Saliu, an AI Product Builder and founder. I design AI-native products — from LLM-powered knowledge systems to internal business platforms — and my rule is simple: if people don't actually use it every day, it doesn't ship.

## How I work

It starts with the messy real-world workflow, not the model. I sit with the problem — how a team really tracks work, how an author really writes, how a VA really spends their hours — and then compose product thinking, AI systems, and rapid prototyping into something clean and production-ready.

## What to expect here

- **Build notes**: lessons from shipping AI products like The Scribe, POD System, MeetSummary AI, and Nonfiction AI.
- **AI workflows**: RAG, voice and knowledge systems, and automation patterns that hold up in production.
- **Product thinking**: strategy, prototyping, and go-to-market for AI-native tools.
